How do you approach building an AI-powered application?

How I approach a build

I work in five stages, from the business question to a solution people use. This is how I approach engineering work. The examples point to documented projects; not every activity applies to every project.

  • 01 Understand the problem Understand business requirements, data sources, constraints and desired outcomes, before choosing any technology. Tools: SQL, Python, Requirement documents. In practice: The Knowledge Portal began from a supplied functional specification, which set out its roles and capabilities. Case study: 01 Understand the problem
  • 02 Design the system Plan the architecture, technology stack, data flow and implementation strategy, so the build has a clear shape. Tools: Snowflake, ETL / ELT, Data Pipelines, Data Warehousing. In practice: In the Data Migration initiative, validation is its own stage between transforming and loading, so problems surface before data reaches Snowflake. Case study: 02 Design the system
  • 03 Build & engineer Develop data pipelines, applications, AI capabilities and automation workflows that people can actually use. Tools: Python, SQL, Snowflake, Databricks, Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, AI Agents. In practice: Business Sheet was built end to end: from chart generation that replaces manual Excel work to the chatbot that explains the metrics. Case study: 03 Build & engineer
  • 04 Test & validate Validate functionality, data quality, performance, accuracy and reliability, and keep people in the loop where AI is involved. Tools: SQL, Model Evaluation, Testing and debugging. In practice: Contract Intelligence presents extracted clauses and obligations as a summary for the legal team to review: it supports their decisions rather than making them. Case study: 04 Test & validate
  • 05 Deploy & improve Deploy the solution, monitor its performance and continuously improve it. Tools: Stakeholder feedback, Iterative releases, Documentation. In practice: Resume Matching began as my proof of concept and is now live in production; the Knowledge Portal has been refined through business feedback. Case study: 05 Deploy & improve
