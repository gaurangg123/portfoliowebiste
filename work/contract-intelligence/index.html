AI Legal Contract Intelligence

An AI-powered application for legal teams to analyze and track uploaded contracts and extract important contractual information.

Where
Tata Technologies
For
Legal teams
Type
Generative AI application

Problem and users

Legal teams need to find the clauses, obligations and key details in each contract, and keep track of their contracts over time. This application analyzes uploaded contracts and pulls that information out for the team to review.

My role

I worked on this application as part of my role at Tata Technologies, where I build AI-powered applications including intelligent document solutions.

How it works

  1. UploadA contract is uploaded to the application.
  2. AnalyzeThe application analyzes it with generative AI.
  3. ExtractIt identifies clauses and extracts obligations and other key information.
  4. TrackContracts are tracked in the application.

Design decision

Support legal review; don’t replace it. The application organizes contract information for legal teams. It doesn’t give legal advice or make legal decisions.
