01The problem

Legal teams need to identify and review the clauses, obligations and important information in each contract, and keep track of their contracts.

02Technical approach

A contract is uploaded and analyzed: its clauses, obligations and key details are extracted into a structured summary for the legal team to review, and the contract is tracked in the application.
