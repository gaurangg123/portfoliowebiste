AI Legal Contract Intelligence

An AI-powered application for legal teams to analyze and track uploaded contracts and extract important contractual information.

Where
Tata Technologies
For
Legal teams
Type
Generative AI application

Business problem

Legal teams need to identify and review the clauses, obligations and important information in each contract, and keep track of their contracts.

How the solution works

  1. UploadA contract is uploaded to the application.
  2. AnalyzeThe application analyzes it with generative AI.
  3. ExtractIt identifies clauses and extracts obligations and other key information.
  4. TrackContracts are tracked in the application.

It supports human review: it organizes contract information for the legal team, and doesn’t give legal advice or make legal decisions.

Result

Legal teams get each contract’s clauses, obligations and key information as a structured summary to review, and their contracts are tracked in the application.

  1. A sample contractA fictional services agreement is uploaded for analysis.
  2. Clauses and obligationsExample clauses, obligations and key details are highlighted in the text.
  3. Structured summaryThe extracted information is organized into a structured summary.
  4. Human reviewThe legal team reviews the summary and makes the decisions. The application doesn’t give legal advice.
