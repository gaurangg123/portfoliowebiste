How I approach the work

This is how I approach engineering work. The examples point to documented projects; not every activity applies to every project.

  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    Understand business requirements, data sources, constraints and desired outcomes, before choosing any technology.

    In practice: The Knowledge Portal began from a supplied functional specification, which set out its roles and capabilities.
  2. 02

    Design the system

    Plan the architecture, technology stack, data flow and implementation strategy, so the build has a clear shape.

    In practice: In the Data Migration initiative, validation is its own stage between transforming and loading, so problems surface before data reaches Snowflake.
  3. 03

    Build & engineer

    Develop data pipelines, applications, AI capabilities and automation workflows that people can actually use.

    In practice: Business Sheet was built end to end: from chart generation that replaces manual Excel work to the chatbot that explains the metrics.
  4. 04

    Test & validate

    Validate functionality, data quality, performance, accuracy and reliability, and keep people in the loop where AI is involved.

    In practice: Contract Intelligence presents extracted clauses and obligations as a summary for the legal team to review: it supports their decisions rather than making them.
  5. 05

    Deploy & improve

    Deploy the solution, monitor its performance and continuously improve it.

    In practice: Resume Matching began as my proof of concept and is now live in production; the Knowledge Portal has been refined through business feedback.

Enterprise Knowledge Portal

One platform for knowledge discovery, requests, an AI chatbot, approvals and leaderboards.

Problem
Make organizational knowledge easier to discover and use through a shared platform with role-based access. It serves four roles: Viewer, User, Admin and Champion.

The portal brings six capabilities into one application: discovery and search, knowledge requests, an approval queue, an AI chatbot, leaderboards, and sharing with role-based access. People use whichever they need, in any order.
