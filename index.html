About

I’m Gaurang, a Data & AI Engineer at Tata Technologies in Pune. I joined in October 2025, after a B.Tech in computer science with a specialization in big data analytics.

My work spans data engineering and applied AI: data pipelines and migration into Snowflake on one side, AI-powered applications such as chatbots, document tools and knowledge platforms on the other. I like taking an idea from data, to AI, to an application people use.

Next, I want to go deeper into AI and automation, especially agentic coding, managing AI agents and tailoring LLMs to specific business use cases.
